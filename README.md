## Projects

Analytics is the focus: take a messy dataset, find the question that matters, turn it into something a stakeholder can act on.

### Analytics

- **[The Ghost Marketplace: C2C Fashion EDA](https://leodid68.github.io/01-the-ghost-marketplace.html)** — a French-founded C2C fashion marketplace with 98,913 users across 200 countries. Activation funnel, six behavioural segments, and the finding that a "registered users" metric hides most of what matters: around 0.5% of users carry most of the activity. Shipped as a Tableau-ready dataset.
- **[Banking Fraud Detection: SQL](https://leodid68.github.io/fraud-sql-analytics.html)** — 480,000 approved transactions across 9 PostgreSQL tables, 7 fraud patterns. Multi-CTE pipelines, window functions, a rule-based risk score, ending in 5 live Looker Studio dashboards on Supabase. One clear result: 92% of card-fraud cases bypassed 3D Secure.
- **[Credit Risk: Early Warning Engine](https://leodid68.github.io/aririsk-walkthrough.html)** — 375,000+ Estonian companies. SQL-first credit scoring (ratios, cohorts, Altman-Z, window functions) plus a probability-of-default model (AUC 0.913).

- **[FDA Adverse Event Analysis](https://leodid68.github.io/01-faers-data-pipeline-eda.html)** — 1.6M adverse drug event reports, severity classification (XGBoost + SHAP), pharmacovigilance signal detection with PRR & ROR.
